Gainsight is a customer success platform that helps companies retain and grow their customer base through data-driven insights, automation and engagement. Four product lines ship developer surfaces: Gainsight CS (the customer success platform, with REST, Bulk, SCIM and a first-party remote MCP server), Gainsight PX (product experience and analytics, the one surface with a published Swagger contract), Gainsight CC / Customer Communities (348 documented REST operations plus a widget, connector and SDK extension platform), and Skilljar (customer education). Authentication is OAuth 2.1 with PKCE on Gainsight CS, OAuth 2.0 client credentials on CC, and an API-key header on PX.

**Areas:** Customer Success · Customer Experience · Product Analytics · Customer Communities · Customer Health · Customer Education · Software-as-a-Service · MCP · Retention · Community

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Gainsight: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.

## Open an issue

- **Something about this listing** — a wrong description, a missing API, a stale artifact or a question about the rating: [open an issue on this repository](https://github.com/api-evangelist/gainsight/issues/new).
- **A new API, or one that is not in the catalog yet** — [submit it on APIs.io](https://apis.io/add/).
- **Anything else** — [the APIs.io inbox](https://github.com/api-search/inbox/issues/new) or [info@apievangelist.com](mailto:info@apievangelist.com).
